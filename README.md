This repository hosts materials to analyse the predictive relationship between the selected social-ecological and perception variables with crop choice amongst rural farming communities in the concerned study area. The research was conducted in selected localities of Bheriganga Municipality (Surkhet), Bhairabi Rural Municipality (Dailekh), and Budhinanda Municipality (Bajura). Machine Learning Algorithms (Random Forest Classifier and Support Vector Machine) were deployed to peform the analysis regarding what variables were signficant or relevant in influencing the decision of the survey respondents in influencing their decision to plant indigenous crops, improved crops (incl. hybrid varieties), and mixed plantations comprising all crop types.
