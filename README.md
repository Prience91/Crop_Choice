This repository hosts materials to analyse the predictive relationship between the selected social-ecological and perception variables with crop choice amongst rural farming communities in the concerned study area. The research was conducted in selected localities of Bheriganga Municipality (Surkhet), Bhairabi Rural Municipality (Dailekh), and Budhinanda Municipality (Bajura) to identify the variables that were relatively significant in influencing the decision of the surveyed households in influencing their decision to plant indigenous crops, improved crops (incl. hybrid varieties), and mixed plantations comprising all crop types. Machine Learning Algorithms (Random Forest Classifier and Support Vector Machine) were deployed with standard preprocessing protocol as discussed in (Shalev-Shwartz & Ben-David, 2014) to avoid cross-contamination, overfitting, and other possible irregularities. 

The research is a combined effort of individuals representing Global Institute for Interdisciplinary Studies (GIIS) and International Center for Integrated Mountain Development (ICIMOD). The research was funded under the Green Resilient Agricultural Productive Ecosystems (GRAPE) initiative that targeted the Karnali and Sudurpaschim Provinces in Nepal.
